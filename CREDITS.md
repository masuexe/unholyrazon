# Credits and Attributions

Unholy Razon is an open-source fork based on **Unholy Unbound (v7b)**, originally branched from the **Unholy Classes & Bosses** project for [Mega Man 8-Bit Deathmatch](https://mm8bdm.net/) (MM8BDM).

This document attributes sprites, skins, mugshots, sound effects, and gameplay assets contributed by community creators across Unholy Razon, Unholy Unbound, and upstream projects.

---
